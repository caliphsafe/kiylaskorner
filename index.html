HOW IT STARTED

MADE IN A
HARD YEAR.

When the pandemic hit, David Bizarro's transportation business lost about 90% of its business. Instead of waiting for things to turn around, the family turned a tough moment into a new beginning.

Kiyla's Korner opened in January 2021 — a colorful neighborhood food spot built around hot dogs, comfort food and getting people together.
