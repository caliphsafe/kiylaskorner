HOW THE KORNER STARTED

Built by family.

Kiyla's Korner opened in 2021 and grew from a family pivot during a difficult moment for local business. Today, the Korner is a neighborhood food-truck spot built around colorful energy, casual outdoor seating and comfort food that doesn't take itself too seriously.
