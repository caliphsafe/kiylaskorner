HOW THE KORNER STARTED

BUILT BY FAMILY.
BUILT IN NEW BEDFORD.

Kiyla's Korner opened in January 2021, during one of the hardest stretches of the pandemic. When David Bizarro's transportation business lost most of its business, the family turned a difficult moment into a new opportunity — a place built around food, family and bringing people together.

What is Kiyla's Korner?+

Kiyla's Korner is a New Bedford neighborhood food destination known for hot dogs, loaded fries, burgers, wraps, shakes and casual outdoor seating.

Where is Kiyla's Korner?+

The restaurant is listed at 85 Osborne Street in New Bedford, Massachusetts, around the Cove Road / Osborne Street area.

What should I try first?+

Start with one of the Korner favorites — a specialty dog, loaded fries, a burger or a shake — then come back and build your own.
